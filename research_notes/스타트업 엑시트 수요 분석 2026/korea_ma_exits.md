# Korean (국내) Startup M&A Exits, 2025–2026

> Research date: 2026-10-29. Method: web search. WebFetch was blocked for several Korean news hosts in this session (hankyung.com and news.nate.com did not resolve), so most facts come from search-result snippets and headlines, not full-article reads. Deal details marked "unverified" should be checked against the original article or DART before publication.

## Q1. What are the most recent (2025–2026) Korean startup acquisitions?

### Takeaway
2026 domestic startup M&A was led by **AI deals between startups** (Upstage buying Daum, Rebellions buying SqueezeBits), **B2B/HR/security tuck-ins** (JobKorea–JobPlanet, Netz–SoftPrick, Day1Company–SpaceY), and **K-beauty brand and ODM roll-ups/PE buyouts** (Goodai Global, Macquarie PE, Seoul Leager). Chaebol and big-platform buyers (Naver, Kakao, Toss, Coupang, Samsung, LG) mostly appeared as **sellers** (Kakao selling Daum, SK Square selling TMAP) or **minority investors** (SK Networks into Upstage, Naver into US startups). I found no confirmed 2026 outright acquisition of a Korean startup by Naver, Kakao, Toss, Coupang, Samsung or LG.

### Inferences
- **Startups and scale-ups are now the main acquirers** of Korean startups (Upstage, Rebellions, Day1Company, The Swing, Delightroom, Goodai Global). The chaebol "acqui-hire" of AI startups that the brief asks about **did not show up in 2026 search results**. Large groups were more often divesting (Kakao→Daum, SK Square→TMAP) or taking minority stakes (SK Networks→Upstage).
- **K-beauty is the dominant sector by deal value.** Goodai Global alone did roughly ₩1tn or more of brand acquisitions in 2025–26, and PE is now moving into ODM/manufacturing.
- **AI deals are mostly vertical "full-stack" integrations** (chip + inference software; portal + LLM; edtech + AX services), not talent-only acqui-hires.
- Many deals are consolidation of weak sub-sectors (PM scooters, content communities, job platforms), not high-multiple exits.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm any 2025–26 outright acquisition of a Korean startup by Naver, Kakao, Toss, Coupang, Samsung, SK, LG, Hyundai, CJ (other than Studio Dragon), Lotte, GS, Shinsegae, KT, LG U+, Krafton, Nexon, NCSoft, Danggeun, Musinsa, Yanolja or Woowa. Searches returned no such deals, but full-text access to Korean outlets was blocked, so some may have been missed. Company-by-company DART searches ("타법인 주식 및 출자증권 취득결정") are needed.
- Deal values for Upstage–Daum, Rebellions–SqueezeBits, JobKorea–JobPlanet, Day1Company–SpaceY and Netz–SoftPrick were not visible in snippets.
- The exact dates of Goodai Global–Skin1004 and Sisso–Publy are unverified.
- The full list of Outstanding's 23 cases for 2025 could not be retrieved; the page was not fetchable.

## Q2. Which sectors had the highest frequency of M&A exits in Korea?

### Takeaway
No 2025–26 sector-by-count breakdown of Korean startup M&A was found. Historical Startup Alliance and Startup Recipe data (through 2023) put **consumer and lifestyle sectors (F&B, beauty, games, content)** first, followed by enterprise/B2B and bio/medical. In 2025–26, coverage points to **K-beauty** (by value and visibility) and **AI/B2B software** (by recent strategic deals) as the most active categories.

### Cited Findings
- **Deal-count trend (Tracxn):** 26 acquisitions of South Korean companies through July 2026, against 45 for all of 2025. This is a snippet-level figure from Tracxn's Korea page, and Tracxn's coverage of Korean deals is incomplete. — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/geographies/south-korea/__us6kb0pJxXgamA9AipJbdHh7DP6AZ-jAWckbXbAmAhQ)
- **Historical counts (Startup Alliance):** 25 startup M&A deals in 2020, 57 in 2021 and 127 in 2022 (2.2× year on year). Another tally shows a peak of 150 in 2022 and 88 in 2023. The two series use different definitions. — [Startup Recipe summary](https://startuprecipe.co.kr/archives/invest-newsletter/5701774); [Hankyung, "스타트업 M&A 반토막"](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2025052945361) **(pre-2025 data)**
- **Sector ranking (Startup Recipe):** M&A was most active in **content, F&B/restaurants, enterprise, games, then bio/medical**. Deal counts rose while total value fell, and a few large deals drove the totals. — [Startup Recipe](https://startuprecipe.co.kr/archives/invest-newsletter/5701774) **(older data, through about 2023)**
- **Sector ranking (Startup Alliance, cited in press):** acquired startups were mostly **F&B, beauty and games**, so consumer and lifestyle sectors led. — [Startup Recipe](https://startuprecipe.co.kr/archives/invest-newsletter/5701774); [Hankyung](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2025052945361) **(older data)**
- **Cosmetics M&A count:** 11 cosmetics-company M&A deals in 2025, including Goodai Global's purchases of Seorin Company and Skinfood. — [CNC News](http://www.cncnews.co.kr/news/article.html?no=10711)
- **Dealsite Q3 2025 league table:** K-beauty, led by Goodai Global, was the main source of activity in an otherwise dry M&A market. — [Dealsite](https://dealsite.co.kr/articles/149953/080058)
- **Who buys:** about 7 in 10 domestic M&A deals involved small and venture companies, and large-company acquisitions were declining. — [Unicorn Factory, Nov 2024](https://www.unicornfactory.co.kr/article/2024110711330739190) **(2024)**
- **SaaS distress M&A:** Hankyung (Jun 2026) reported that SaaS companies are turning to M&A to survive, a sign of B2B SaaS consolidation. Full text was not accessible. — [Hankyung](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2026061973871)
- **Venture-to-venture deals:** a Sept 2026 report noted money flowing into unlisted bio ventures and M&A between ventures and startups. Full text was not accessible. — [Nate/News, Sept 13, 2026](https://news.nate.com/view/20260913n13352)
- **Wider Korean M&A market:** 737 deals in H1 2025 (Samil PwC), down 10% year on year; both count and value fell by double digits. This covers all M&A, not just startups. — [Asiae CORE](https://core.asiae.co.kr/article/2025080809150609960)

### Inferences
- By **count**, the long-run Korean pattern is consumer, F&B, content and games, because these are small, cash-generating businesses that are easy for strategic buyers or holdcos to absorb. By **value** in 2025–26, **K-beauty** leads clearly. **AI/B2B SaaS** is the fastest-rising category in 2026 headlines.
- Rough frequency ranking for 2025–26, based on the deals above plus historical data (a qualitative judgment, not a measured statistic): (1) beauty/K-beauty brands and ODM, (2) AI and B2B SaaS/AX, (3) content/IP/media, (4) HR tech and career platforms, (5) mobility/PM, (6) security, (7) F&B franchises (historically high; few 2025–26 examples found), (8) bio/healthcare.

### Gaps
- No 2025 or 2026 sector-by-count statistic for Korean startup M&A was found from Startup Alliance, 혁신의숲, THE VC, KVCA or MSS (중기부). THE VC publishes monthly investment statistics (e.g., Aug 2026: 78 deals, ₩656.9bn invested) but no M&A sector breakdown surfaced. — [THE VC](https://thevc.kr/discussions/korea_startup_funding_2026_08)
- Healthcare/medical AI, fintech, proptech, edtech and logistics deals for 2025–26 were not found in searches. This may reflect search coverage rather than true absence.

## Q3. Notable trends: K-beauty roll-ups and PE buyouts, AI acqui-hires, and M&A share vs IPO in VC exits

### Takeaway
Korean VC exits still depend heavily on IPO and secondary share sales (구주매각). **M&A is only 2.3% of mother-fund (모태 자펀드) recovery by amount in 2025.** Policy and industry voices in 2026 are pushing M&A and secondaries as the fix. K-beauty valuations of 17–20× EBITDA are drawing both PE and strategic roll-ups, and capital is shifting toward manufacturing and ODM.

### Cited Findings
**Exit mix (M&A vs IPO vs sale):**
- In 2025, **M&A was only 2.3% (by amount)** of the mother-fund sub-funds' recovery. About 90.6% came from IPO and off-market share sales. — [Money Today, Aug 26, 2026](https://www.mt.co.kr/future/2026/08/26/2026082415200846804); [Unicorn Factory](https://www.unicornfactory.co.kr/article/2026082415200846804)
- The IPO share of VC exits rose from **24.3% (2022) to 37.3% (2025) and 37.9% (H1 2026)**. The "sale" share (which includes M&A, off-market sales, buyouts and share buybacks) fell from **56.5% (2022) to 47.7% (H1 2026)**. — [Money Today](https://www.mt.co.kr/future/2026/08/26/2026082415200846804)
- For comparison, M&A's share of exits is **47.3% in the US and 46.4% in Europe**. PitchBook's figures for Apr 2025–Mar 2026 put US VC exits at M&A 44.1% and secondaries 29.2%. Startup Alliance has claimed about **94%** of US VC exits are M&A, which conflicts with the other figures and likely reflects a different definition. — [Money Today](https://www.mt.co.kr/future/2026/08/26/2026082415200846804); [Junggi Economy](https://www.junggi.co.kr/article/articleView.html?no=34581)
- H1 2026 venture investment was **₩8.8tn (+56%)**. Recovery was **38% IPO and 48% sale**. — [Supple](https://supple.kr/news/cmtrzx29o004a1354i600e0oz)
- KVCA: M&A was **38.0% of Q1 startup recovery, the lowest in five years.** The year is ambiguous; the source article is likely from 2025. — cited via [Hankyung](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2025052945361) / [Startup Recipe search context](https://startuprecipe.co.kr/archives/invest-newsletter/5701774). *Note: this 38% "M&A" figure conflicts with the 2.3% mother-fund figure. The KVCA category probably bundles M&A with secondary sales.*
- Another outlet estimates the IPO dependence of Korean VC recovery at about 85% and secondaries at about 5%, against about 24% in major overseas markets. Its methodology is unclear. — [KJob News](https://www.kjob.news/news/531608)
- VC exits are crowding into IPOs, but 8 of 10 candidates are blocked by weak earnings. — [FN News, Sept 8, 2026](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202609081356499043)
- Stricter KOSDAQ listing and maintenance standards make M&A a more realistic exit route. — [KPMG CFO Lounge via Nate, Oct 21, 2026](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20261021n39665)
- The industry response to the government's secondary-fund plan: "The real fix is M&A." — [Money Today, Aug 27, 2026](https://www.mt.co.kr/future/2026/08/27/2026082622505993506)
- Korea's three main entrepreneurship academic societies urged regional startups to use M&A as an exit (Apr 2026). — [FN News](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202604201631441674)

**K-beauty roll-ups and PE:**
- K-beauty brand owners trade at **17–18×, up to 20× or more EBITDA**, against **14.9×** for global beauty M&A last year (Capstone Partners). Blackstone valued Juno Hair at about ₩800bn. PE and VC firms aim to become a "K-beauty mafia." — [Hankyung, Jul 2, 2026](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2026070226401)
- Foreign PEs are pursuing mid-size K-brand deals worth several hundred billion won. Domestic PEs have become cautious because of regulation, public-opinion risk and valuation. — [InvestChosun, Feb 5, 2026](https://www.investchosun.com/site/data/html_dir/2026/02/05/2026020580191.html)
- PE and SI capital is moving from brands to **ODM/OEM and packaging** (Macquarie for Hwaseong Cosmetic and Nowcos; EcoPro with KIP). — [Economist KR, Aug 18, 2026](https://economist.co.kr/article/view/ecn202608180028); [FN News](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202608131055178807)
- Goodai Global (TirTir, Skinfood, Round Lab, Skin1004) built roughly ₩1.5tn in revenue (2025) through serial M&A and is now an IPO candidate. — [NewDaily, Apr 13, 2026](https://biz.newdaily.co.kr/site/data/html/2026/04/13/2026041300310.html); [Edaily Marketin](https://marketin.edaily.co.kr/News/Read?newsId=03489926642171544); [Bloter](https://www.bloter.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=640373)

**AI M&A:**
- Domestic AI M&A in 2026 was led by startups: Upstage bought Daum, and Rebellions bought SqueezeBits (see Q1 table).
- A May 2026 ecosystem analysis expects Naver, Kakao, LG and Samsung to absorb AI startups. This is a **forecast, not confirmed deals**. — [TechPulse](https://techpulse.co.kr/2026-05-15-korea-ai-startup-ecosystem/) (low-authority source)
- Globally, AI startups buying AI startups reached 195 deals this year. This is a global figure, not a Korean one. — [Junggi Economy](https://www.junggi.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=38065)

### Inferences
- Korean VC exits remain structurally dependent on IPOs. Strategic M&A is a small share of VC recovery by value even where the "sale" category looks large, because that category is mostly secondary share sales and buybacks. Rising listing standards in 2026 and the IPO bottleneck are pushing policy discussion toward M&A, but deal flow has not yet caught up.
- K-beauty is the one category where buyers will pay premium multiples (17–20× EBITDA). That makes it the clearest current exit market for Korean consumer startups.
- The AI acqui-hire-by-chaebol thesis is **not supported by 2026 evidence found**. AI consolidation is happening between startups and through chaebol minority stakes.

### Gaps
- No single authoritative 2025 full-year figure for M&A's share of Korean VC recovery was found. The available figures (2.3% for the mother fund; about 38% for KVCA Q1 with an unclear year and definition; about 48% "sale" in the portal data) use different definitions.
- No data was found on how many 2025–26 Korean startup acquisitions were acqui-hires.
- Full texts from Hankyung, Nate and some others could not be fetched in this session (host blocked), so several 2026 analyses are cited from headline and snippet only.
