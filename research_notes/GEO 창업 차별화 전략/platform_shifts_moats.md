## 1. Platform changes 2025–2026 (ads, agentic commerce, product feeds) and what they mean for organic GEO

### Takeaway
AI engines have now built both of the things that threaten a "pure visibility" business. Paid placement exists: ChatGPT Ads runs in 89 countries, including Korea, with Pixel/CAPI, product feeds and CPA bidding. Merchant feeds exist too: OpenAI's ACP feed spec and Google Merchant Center/UCP. In-chat checkout has proven harder than expected. OpenAI retired Instant Checkout in March 2026, and Google's UCP checkout is still limited to select US/CA/AU merchants. The opportunity for merchants is therefore shifting from "rank in the answer" to "be machine-legible": clean feeds, structured facts, and accurate stock, with checkout staying on the merchant site.

Beta self-serve Ads Manager and CPC arrived May 5. **OpenAI Instant Checkout / ACP / product feeds**
- Instant Checkout launched Sep 29, 2025 with US Etsy sellers. ACP was published the same day under Apache 2.0, co-developed by Stripe and OpenAI. From Jan 26, 2026, Shopify merchants paid OpenAI a 4% fee on ChatGPT checkout sales. In early March 2026 The Information reported native checkout was gone, and on Mar 24, 2026 OpenAI said merchants would use their own checkout, because the first version "did not offer the level of flexibility that we aspire to provide". About a dozen Shopify merchants went live (The Information), or about 30 per Shopify to Forrester. — [INITE summary citing Stripe, TechCrunch, PYMNTS, Forrester (opened)](https://inite.ai/en/blog/agentic-commerce-after-instant-checkout)
- INITE attributes the failure to sales tax, fraud signals living with the merchant, and real-time inventory accuracy. This is the author's analysis, not OpenAI's. — [INITE](https://inite.ai/en/blog/agentic-commerce-after-instant-checkout)
- OpenAI's current commerce docs describe ACP as "the connective layer between merchants and ChatGPT users", letting ChatGPT "ingest structured catalog data, understand merchant inventory". — [OpenAI Commerce docs (opened)](https://developers.openai.com/commerce)
- **Feed spec (key structural fact):** one product feed drives three separate eligibilities: organic search/discovery (`is_eligible_search`, default true), Ads (`is_ads_eligible`, default off), and checkout (`is_eligible_checkout`, default off, which needs a separately enabled integration plus privacy and ToS URLs). The shared format currently represents only US, CA and MX destination countries, and "standard uploads use ["US"] regardless". It includes variant modeling, shipping and returns. Raw reviews and Q&A are not part of the discovery feed. — [OpenAI feed spec (opened)](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/feed)

**Google AI Mode / UCP / AP2 / Universal Cart**
- UCP is an open standard for agentic commerce from discovery to post-purchase, compatible with A2A, AP2 and MCP. A "Buy" button appears in AI Mode and Gemini only for listings with the `native_commerce(checkout_eligibility)` attribute. The merchant remains seller of record, payments run through Google Pay/Wallet stored cards (FPANs), and it is available "for select merchants at this time", for products eligible in the **US, Canada, and Australia** only. Merchant Center remains "the central hub", and the best preparation is robust, up-to-date feeds. Google separates UCP from its older "agentic checkout", which buys on the merchant's own site. — [Google Merchant Center Help (opened)](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/16837055?hl=en)
- UCP is expanding to Lodging and Food. Upcoming: multi-item carts, loyalty account linking, post-purchase tracking. It uses existing Merchant Center shopping feeds. — [Google for Developers UCP (opened)](https://developers.google.com/merchant/ucp)
- Universal Cart was announced at I/O in May 2026: a cross-merchant cart across Search, Gemini, and later YouTube and Gmail, with price history, back-in-stock alerts and incompatibility warnings. It uses the shopper's payment, perks and loyalty data to find best deals. Checkout is via Google Pay or a hand-off to the merchant site, with "the brand stays the merchant of record". Launch partners include Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta, Walmart and Wayfair. UCP and AP2 underpin it. — [Retail Dive (opened)](https://retaildive.com/news/google-launches-cross-retailer-universal-cart/820957/). US availability in Search and Gemini around Oct 15–16, 2026 — [NewsBytes / AndroidHeadlines (snippet-only)](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/10/google-universal-cart-shopping-launch.html)
- Wharton's Kartik Hosanagar: "Whoever controls the agents now has the power", with the risk that retailers lose customer data to the middleman. — [Retail Dive](https://retaildive.com/news/google-launches-cross-retailer-universal-cart/820957/)

**Amazon / Perplexity**

Features include auto-buy at a target price, Scheduled Actions, and "Buy for Me" at other retailers. **Korea-specific platform context**
- Naver fully ended its "related searches" feature on Apr 30, 2026, replacing it with "AI Briefing" and the "AI tab". — [The Sync newsletter (maily, opened)](https://maily.so/thesync/posts/knrj1vl0rld)

### Inferences
- **Ads plus feeds expand GEO's scope rather than shrinking it.** The same ChatGPT feed drives organic and paid eligibility, and Google uses Merchant Center for both. Feed quality therefore becomes a shared input to organic visibility, ads and checkout. The "organic-only visibility tracker" category is the one squeezed, because engines will sell impression and conversion reporting to advertisers directly.
- Instant Checkout's retirement means "agentic checkout integration" is a weak near-term wedge, especially in Korea where neither the ACP feed (US/CA/MX) nor UCP checkout (US/CA/AU) is available. **Machine-legible product data** (structured facts, real-time stock, policies, canonical URLs) is a no-regret wedge that works across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Naver.
- Korea timing: ChatGPT Ads went live in Korea in August 2026 and is self-serve. Korean advertisers will soon want help combining paid and organic AI visibility, and Korean-language measurement. Naver's AI Briefing is a Korea-only surface that global tools are unlikely to cover well.

### Gaps
- OpenAI's own posts (openai.com) could not be opened. Korea-specific ad formats, minimums and KRW billing are unverified.
- No primary source found on whether ChatGPT Shopping product results (organic carousels) are live in Korea, or on Korean-language feed support.
- No data found on the share of ChatGPT product answers that are organic vs. sponsored.
- Status of AP2 adoption beyond Google's own stack is not researched in depth.

## 2. How answer engines select sources now (citations, fan-out, schema, llms.txt, variance)

### Takeaway
Citations concentrate on a small set of high-authority and community domains (Wikipedia, Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, review sites), and the mix differs sharply by engine. Google says there is no special schema or optimization needed for AI Overviews/AI Mode, which use query fan-out over its index. llms.txt is effectively unused by crawlers. Individual answers are close to random lists: fewer than 1 in 100 repeats give the same brand list. Only visibility percentage across many samples is statistically meaningful.

### Cited Findings
**Original GEO paper**
- Aggarwal et al., "GEO: Generative Engine Optimization" (arXiv 2311.09735, v3 Jun 28 2024, KDD 2024) introduced GEO-bench. It found that GEO methods "can boost visibility by up to 40%" in generative engine responses, and that efficacy "varies across domains". — [arXiv (opened)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735)

**Citation patterns (studies disagree; vendor data)**
- Profound (680M citations, Aug 2024–Jun 2025): Wikipedia is ChatGPT's top source at 7.8% of total citations (47.9% of its top-10 share). Reddit leads Google AI Overviews (2.2%) and Perplexity (6.6%). Perplexity favors community content, while AIO is more distributed (LinkedIn matters more). — [Profound (opened)](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ai-platform-citation-patterns)
- Similarweb (about 600K US citation events, Jan–Feb 2026): ChatGPT cites Wikipedia 13.15% and Reddit 11.97%. Kevin Indig/SEL (Mar 2026): about 30 domains capture about 67% of citations in a topic, and the top 10 take about 46% in product-comparison topics. Pages ranking #1 in Google were cited by ChatGPT 43.2% of the time (3.5x those beyond the top 20), and 85% of retrieved pages got zero citations. Ahrefs (Oct 2025): 29.7% of ChatGPT's top 1,000 cited pages are Wikipedia, and 65.3% of cited pages that rank in Google are on DR81+ domains. Semrush: Reddit's share of ChatGPT responses swung from about 60% to about 10% in roughly six weeks in 2025. SE Ranking: cross-engine domain overlap is only 9.8%–25.2%. — [Trendly AI roundup (opened; secondary aggregation)](https://blog.trendlyai.com/most-cited-domains-chatgpt)
- Contently roundup: Evertune (200M prompts) found the top domain rarely exceeds 5% of citations, and about 95% are spread across thousands of domains. Semrush (325K prompts) found LinkedIn cited in 14.3% of ChatGPT Search, 13.5% of AI Mode and 5.3% of Perplexity responses. Ahrefs (75K brands, Dec 2025): YouTube mentions are the strongest correlate of AI visibility (r=0.737). SE Ranking (129K domains): presence on review sites (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Yelp) means 4.6–6.3 vs 1.8 citations, and heavy Reddit mention presence means 7 vs 1.8 ChatGPT citations. — [Contently, 2026-04-29 (opened; secondary)](https://contently.com/2026/04/29/top-sources-llms-cite/)
- Search summary: Ahrefs July 2026 has Reddit (16.7%) overtaking Wikipedia (8.9%) in ChatGPT, and Peec AI (30M sources) has Reddit first across engines. — [SEL on Peec study **(snippet-only; SEL blocked)**](https://searchengineland.com/ai-search-engines-cite-reddit-youtube-and-linkedin-most-study-473138)
- Similarweb: citation presence in US ChatGPT prompts rose from about 1.6% (Jun 2025) to about 6.8% (May 2026). Travel is about 23%, Automotive about 20%, and Professional Services under 4%. Categories with "comparable, checkable" options get cited more. — [Similarweb (opened)](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/marketing/geo/gen-ai-stats/)

**Google's official position / fan-out**
- "There are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary". "There's also no special schema.org structured data that you need to add". AIO and AI Mode "may use a 'query fan-out' technique — issuing multiple related searches across subtopics and data sources". To be a supporting link, a page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet. — [Google Search Central: AI features (opened)](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

**llms.txt**
- Limy (515,382,577 LLM bot events over 90 days): only 408 hit /llms.txt. SE Ranking (300K domains) found 10.13% adoption. Google's Gary Illyes (Jul 2025) said Google doesn't support it, and Mueller compared it to the keywords meta tag. No major LLM provider has committed to using it. Limy (a vendor) reframes it as "Business-to-Agent" infrastructure for agents. — [Limy (opened; vendor)](https://limy.ai/blog/llms-txt-in-2026-the-full-guide)
- Prosopo (IP-verified logs): in 4 days, 120 robots.txt fetches, 43 sitemap fetches and 0 llms.txt fetches. Across 6–17 Oct 2026, 7,585 verified AI fetches included 511 robots.txt, 143 sitemaps, 3 llms.txt (GPTBot, Claude Code acting for a user, ClaudeBot) and 1 llms-full.txt. Spoofed AI user-agents from Google Cloud IPs were probing /.env and similar paths, so verify crawlers by IP, not user-agent. AI crawlers concentrated on analyst explainers and structured pricing comparisons. — [Prosopo (opened)](https://prosopo.io/blog/llms-txt/)

**Variance / reproducibility**
- SparkToro + Gumshoe (Jan 28, 2026): 600 volunteers ran 12 prompts through ChatGPT, Claude and Google AI 2,961 times. There is a <1 in 100 chance of the same brand list twice, and about 1 in 1,000 for the same order. List length varies from 2–3 to 10+. Yet top brands appear in 55–77% of responses even across 142 human-written prompts with a mean semantic similarity of only 0.081. Visibility % "across dozens to hundreds of prompts run multiple times is a reasonable metric", but ranking position is not. To know an AI's set, "ask over and over again; usually at least 60–100X". Open questions: required sample size, whether API outputs equal UI outputs (early data suggests caching is not a major problem), and prompt coverage. The partner (Gumshoe) is a tracking vendor, and the post itself discloses the conflict. — [SparkToro (opened)](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/)

### Inferences
- Selection is mainly retrieval plus authority. Google rank, domain authority, and off-site presence (Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, review sites, Wikipedia) dominate on-page "GEO tricks". Fix-it products should therefore focus on off-site and third-party presence and on retrievability (indexing, snippet eligibility), not schema or llms.txt hacks.
- Cross-engine overlap is low (10–25%), so per-engine measurement is required. That multiplies sampling cost and is a natural scale moat.
- Statistical rigor (confidence intervals on visibility %, published methodology) is an under-served differentiator. SparkToro explicitly calls for vendors to "show their math".
- The academic +40% result came from a controlled benchmark. Production engines rely on retrieval and authority, so on-page rewriting lift in the real world is unproven at scale (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No independent, large-scale causal study (randomized content changes leading to citation lift) in production engines was found. Most evidence is correlational vendor data.
- KDD follow-up papers (e.g., adversarial GEO, ranking manipulation studies) were not opened in this pass.
- No Korean-language citation-pattern study was found (e.g., which Korean domains such as Naver Blog, Namuwiki, Tistory, or community sites ChatGPT/Gemini cite for Korean queries). This is a real data gap and a possible wedge.
- Freshness effects were not quantified from an opened source.

## 3. AI crawler/agent traffic, pay-per-crawl, and "agent experience" (AXP) risk

### Takeaway
AI bots are a material and rising share of traffic (about 5.3% of Cloudflare requests in Sep 2026, plus 3.6% from mixed-purpose crawlers). Crawl-to-referral ratios vary widely and move as platforms add citation links. Cloudflare's pay-per-crawl (HTTP 402) is still a closed beta. Serving bot-specific page versions (Scrunch Agent Pages/AXP) is commercially live, but its policy standing rests on the vendor's own interpretation.

### Cited Findings
- Pay per crawl: a site owner sets a flat per-zone price. A crawler either presents payment intent in headers (HTTP 200) or receives HTTP 402 with pricing. Cloudflare is Merchant of Record, and WAF/bot blocks override "charge". It is still in a beta program (docs updated Jul 28, 2026). — [Cloudflare docs (opened)](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-crawl-control/features/pay-per-crawl/what-is-pay-per-crawl/); original launch post Jul 1, 2025, using Web Bot Auth to prevent spoofing — [Cloudflare blog (opened)](https://blog.cloudflare.com/introducing-pay-per-crawl/)
- Cloudflare Radar crawl-to-refer, Sep 2026 (via SEOmator, which pulled the Radar API): Perplexity 2,373:1 (112 in Jan), Anthropic 549:1 (47,779:1 in Jan), OpenAI 341:1, Google 4.7:1, DuckDuckGo 3.6:1, and Mistral with no measurable referrals. AI bots were 5.3% of Cloudflare-classified requests, with mixed-purpose crawlers like Googlebot another 3.6%. Ratios fall when a platform ships citation links. — [SEOmator GEO Data Report (opened)](https://seomator.com/blog/crawl-to-refer-ratio-ai-crawlers-llm-bots)
- Reported but unverified: Cloudflare is shifting toward "pay per use" (paying when content shapes an answer), and a default-block policy timing is said to be Sep 15, 2026. — [search summaries, **snippet-only**](https://valueaddvc.com/pulse/cloudflare-pay-per-crawl-ai-publishers-policy-2026)
- Training crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended) differ from live retrieval agents (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, PerplexityBot). Blocking retrieval agents drops a site "out of answers today". — [Prosopo](https://prosopo.io/blog/llms-txt/)
- Scrunch "Agent Pages" (formerly AXP) serves JavaScript-free optimized HTML only to LLM retrieval bots, not Googlebot or Bingbot. Scrunch claims it "is not considered cloaking" and won't be penalized. — [Scrunch FAQ (opened; vendor claim)](https://scrunch.com/faqs/is-scrunch-agent-experience-platform-axp-considered-cloaking). Detection runs at the CDN layer (Akamai, Cloudflare, Vercel) — [Scrunch help (snippet-only)](https://helpcenter.scrunchai.com/en/articles/13656392-agent-experience-platform-axp)
- Google's cloaking definition: "presenting different content to users and search engines with the intent to manipulate search rankings and mislead users". — [Google spam policies (opened)](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies). Third parties call bot-specific serving "cloaking-adjacent" and an "unsettled tactic". — [Alhena comparison (snippet-only)](https://alhena.ai/blog/alhena-vs-scrunch-ai/). Scrunch is reported as part of Sitecore — [Alhena (snippet-only, unverified)](https://alhena.ai/blog/alhena-vs-scrunch-ai/)

### Inferences
- The agent-experience layer (CDN/edge middleware that serves clean, factual, JS-free versions to verified AI agents) is technically easy for a Claude-Code-heavy team. However, it is already productized (Scrunch/Sitecore) and its policy risk is unresolved: OpenAI and Perplexity have not published rules on it. A safer variant serves **the same content in a more parseable form**, such as server-side rendering, Markdown alternates and structured product facts, with no bot-only claims.
- IP-verified bot analytics are a real, under-served need. Prosopo shows user-agent spoofing is common. A log-based "which AI agents fetched which pages, and did it lead to referrals" product is defensible if it gets server/CDN log access.
- Korean hosting stacks (Cafe24, Imweb, Naver SmartStore, Godomall) are not Cloudflare/Vercel-first, so global AXP tools may not plug in. This is a possible local integration wedge (unverified; see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No OpenAI, Perplexity or Anthropic policy statement on bot-specific content serving was found.
- No data on AI-bot share for Korean sites or Korean CDNs.
- Cloudflare's own Radar page and the pay-per-use announcement were not opened directly.

## 4. Measurement and attribution: can AI visibility be tied to traffic or revenue?

### Takeaway
AI referral traffic is small but growing and fragmenting. ChatGPT's share is falling while Claude and Gemini rise. It engages better than organic search, but conversion claims conflict. OpenAI is building attribution for its *ads* (Pixel/CAPI, incrementality partners), not for organic mentions. Organic-mention-to-revenue attribution remains unsolved and is a genuine open problem.

### Cited Findings
- Similarweb: ChatGPT's share of gen-AI web visits fell from about 76% (Jun 2025) to about 53% (May 2026). Gemini rose to about 27–28% and Claude from about 2% to about 9%. Category visits are 9.5B per month (+70% YoY). Homepage referrals from ChatGPT rose from about 26–29% to about 62–63% of its referrals after an update, which means deep-link attribution gets harder. — [Similarweb (opened)](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/marketing/geo/gen-ai-stats/)
- Goodie (GA4 data, 41 brand sites, B2B): ChatGPT's share of AI referrals fell from 89.1% (May–Aug 2025) to 62.6% (Mar–Apr 2026). Claude reached 18.5% (27.2% raw in April), Gemini 10.6%, Perplexity 7.3% and Copilot about 4%. AI sessions engage about 30% longer than Google organic. — [Goodie AI report (opened; vendor)](https://higoodie.com/blog/ai-search-traffic-report-2026/)
- Conversion rates conflict: ChatGPT referrals at 7.1% (Similarweb clickstream, as summarized), AI at 11.4% vs organic 5.3%, a Seer case study at 15.9%, and a 973-site study finding organic outperforming ChatGPT by about 13% for transactions. — [search summary of aggregator blogs, **snippet-only**](https://www.cognizo.ai/blog/chatgpt-ai-visibility-statistic). **Treat as directional only.**
- ChatGPT Ads native reporting includes a single Conversions metric, and referrer stripping undercounts clicks in analytics. — [Digital Applied ledger](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/where-chatgpt-ads-are-sold-every-market-and-date). OpenAI is "building out measurement alongside the expansion". — [AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/marketers/chatgpt-ads-is-expanding-to-new-markets-and-exploring-new-ad-formats/)
- Google suggests using Search Console plus analytics conversion and time-on-site tracking for AI features traffic. AI Overview/AI Mode clicks are not broken out separately in that guidance. — [Google Search Central (opened)](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

### Inferences
- Attribution is the most valuable unsolved layer. The engines own the paid side. The organic side (a mention leading to a branded search, direct visit or purchase) needs first-party data joins: GA4/Shopify orders, server logs, branded-search lift and geo holdouts. A product that joins *sampled visibility %* with *first-party outcomes* (logs, Cafe24/Shopify orders, Naver Search Advisor) is harder to copy than a dashboard.
- Fragmentation toward Claude and Gemini reduces the value of any single engine's own analytics, which makes neutral multi-engine measurement more defensible.

### Gaps
- No primary source opened on GA4's AI-assistant channel grouping or on ChatGPT's `utm_source=chatgpt.com` behavior in 2026.
- No reliable, primary conversion-rate study for AI referrals (all were snippet-level aggregator claims).
- No Korean data on AI referral share or conversion.

## 5. Moat candidates and threats

### Takeaway
Plain "visibility dashboards" are commoditizing and threatened from two sides: engines selling their own ads analytics, and SEO incumbents bundling. More durable angles combine (a) statistically rigorous, large-scale, local-language answer datasets, (b) closed-loop fixes on the levers that actually move citations (off-site presence, retrievability, feed/product-fact quality), and (c) first-party attribution or integration lock-in (commerce platforms, agencies). For a Korean team, Korean-language and Naver/Kakao surface coverage plus Korean commerce-stack integrations is the clearest gap. No evidence was found of global vendors covering it.

### Cited Findings
- Market and tool evidence: more than $100M per year is estimated to be spent on AI visibility tracking, and the leading critique is that vendors don't publish their stats. — [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/)
- Sampling-cost moat: valid metrics need 60–100 runs per prompt, multiple prompts per intent, and per-engine runs. — [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/). Cross-engine overlap is only 10–25%. — [Trendly roundup](https://blog.trendlyai.com/most-cited-domains-chatgpt)

### Inferences (ranked moat assessment for a 3-person Claude-Code-heavy Korean team)
1. **Korean-language, multi-surface answer dataset with published statistics** (ChatGPT, Gemini/AI Mode, Claude, Perplexity, *Naver AI Briefing*). The moat comes from local surfaces and language, historical time series, and open methodology with confidence intervals. Risk: sampling cost and engine ToS (scraping UIs vs APIs; API-vs-UI equivalence is still an open question per SparkToro).
2. **Closed-loop "diagnose → fix → measure lift" agent on proven levers.** Retrievability and index checks; off-site presence gaps on the Korean equivalents of Reddit/YouTube/review sites (Naver Blog/Cafe, YouTube, Namuwiki, Korean review platforms; *assumption, not evidenced*); product-fact and feed hygiene. Lift measured statistically with holdouts. Claude Code fits auto-generating fixes. The defensibility is the outcome data (which fixes moved visibility), which a dashboard competitor lacks.
3. **Korean commerce-stack integration** (Cafe24, Imweb, Naver SmartStore, Shopify-KR) for machine-legible product data and feeds (Google Merchant Center, OpenAI feed when it expands beyond US/CA/MX), plus IP-verified AI-bot log analytics. App-store distribution works as a channel moat.
4. **Agency/white-label channel.** Korean agencies are actively selling GEO but lack measurement rigor. Supplying the data and reporting layer to them avoids competing on services.
5. **Weaker:** a generic global visibility dashboard (crowded, with engine ads analytics encroaching), llms.txt/schema-only optimization (no evidence of effect), bot-only content serving (policy risk, already productized), and agentic-checkout plumbing for Korea (not available in Korea; Instant Checkout retired).
- Biggest threats: (a) OpenAI/Google exposing organic share-of-voice or product-level insights inside Ads Manager or Merchant Center. Not yet seen, but advertisers are demanding prompt-level reporting. (b) SEO suites bundling AI visibility at near-zero marginal price. (c) Engines tightening ToS or anti-bot measures against UI scraping.
