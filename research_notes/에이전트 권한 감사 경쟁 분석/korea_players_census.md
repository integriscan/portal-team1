# Korean Players in AI Agent Permission Control, MCP Security, Agent Audit, LLM Guardrails and AI Red-Teaming (census as of 2026-10-29)

Method note: direct page fetches were blocked in this session (boannews.com and etnews.com failed DNS resolution). Every fact below comes from **WebSearch result snippets and search-engine summaries**, not from reading full articles. Treat all rows as **snippet-level** evidence. Dates written as "N days ago" in snippets were converted using today's date, 2026-10-29. Product claims are vendor statements unless marked otherwise.

Proximity scale used in the "Closeness" column (closeness to "AI agent permission control + audit trail"):
- **A (direct)**: runtime control of agent tool calls, MCP or API calls, per-agent identity and least privilege, and logging of who or which agent did what.
- **B (adjacent)**: agent identity or IAM, or an AI gateway or platform that includes agent permissions or governance, but runtime tool-call audit is unclear or still on the roadmap.
- **C (guardrail/DLP)**: prompt and response filtering, genAI DLP, shadow-AI control. Mostly content-level, not action-level.
- **D (red-team/eval)**: offensive testing and evaluation. No runtime control.
- **R (roadmap/R&D only)**: announced direction, government project, or patent, with no shipping product evidenced.

## Q1. Census: who in Korea is building agent permission control, MCP security, agent audit, guardrails or red-teaming?

### Takeaway
The space is **not empty**. As of late October 2026 at least 8 Korean companies publicly describe products or near-products that control agent actions or tool calls, MCP calls, or agent identity with logging: QueryPie, SoftCamp, Kuntech, RaonSecure, Netz+Softfreak, Tynapse, AIM Intelligence (Starport) and Samsung SDS FabriX 2.0. A further 10 or more ship genAI guardrails or DLP: Fasoo, Eroun&Company, Jiransoft, ITCEN Cloit, MonitorLab, LG U+, KT, Kakao, Samsung SDS SGuard, S2W and Polaris Office. Red-teaming and evaluation is covered by AIM Intelligence, SelectStar, SK Shieldus, ENKI Whitehat and Eroun. Most "A-class" offerings were **announced between mid-2026 and October 2026** and lack public customer references. That is likely why an earlier search saw "only QueryPie and SoftCamp".

## Q2. Which Korean government programs and regulators fund or shape this area?

### Takeaway
2026 brought concrete public pull: the **Financial Security Institute's AI-agent evaluation criteria** (Oct 2026), which explicitly test tool-permission control and human approval; **KISA/MSIT AI threat manual and red-teaming guide** (July 2026); KISA's AI-security company grants, which funded LLM-guardrail and API-security startups; a **government physical-AI and agent identity R&D project** led by RaonSecure; and the **N2SF** rollout, which is pulling SoftCamp, MonitorLab and Fasoo into genAI and agent control for the public sector.

### Cited Findings
- On 2026-10-07 the Financial Security Institute (금융보안원) released "금융분야 AI 에이전트 보안 평가기준". It has 17 check items, reported as 6 areas (one outlet says 5). Items include goal integrity, cascading-failure preparedness, **tool-permission control, sandbox and human approval systems**, and agent-to-agent communication security. Checks expand from the model layer to the **execution layer**. It will be piloted through year-end, become a formal check standard next year, and be used for financial-sector AI red-teaming. Final criteria will appear in the 2026 "AI Red Team Report". — [Newsis](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20261007_0003816900); [FN](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202610071821048238); [Etoday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2633254)
- On 2026-07-08 MSIT and KISA published an "AI 보안 위협 대응 매뉴얼" and an "AI 보안 레드티밍 가이드". The red-teaming guide is based on ISO/IEC 42119-7 and includes checklists, tools, a red-team job description and 8 industry scenarios. A separate "AI 보안 안내서" was issued in Dec 2025. — [MT](https://www.mt.co.kr/tech/2026/07/08/2026070809202658998); [CELA](https://www.cela.kr/4/?bmode=view&idx=169851178)
- KISA 2025 AI-security-company program:
  - Prototype grants: DBNtech, ENKI Whitehat, **Softfreak (API security monitoring)**, Nurilab, AI Spera.
  - Commercialization grants: EdgeDX, Cyter, SafeAI, GhostPass.
  - Zero-trust pilot leads: Initech, SGA Solutions, SK Shieldus, PrivateTech, **MonitorLab**, ESTsecurity. — [MT 2025-05-09](https://www.mt.co.kr/tech/2025/05/09/2025050917594535446)
- KISA 2026 program: ₩12.04bn, 18 projects, 50 companies. AI-security grantees include **Ailys Frontier + Goorm Lab (LLM guardrail)**, Nurilab and Igloo (agentic SOC). ENKI Whitehat and Sparrow received commercialization grants. — [etnews 2026-05-08](https://www.etnews.com/20260508000063); [KISA](https://www.kisa.or.kr/402/form?postSeq=2590)
- RaonSecure was selected (Oct 2026) as lead of a government **physical-AI security R&D project**: identity and permission management for robots and agents using DID, with SSenStone, Tilon and universities. — [FN 2026-10-15](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202610150924562649)
- MSIT selected a Naver Cloud consortium (32 orgs) for the **cybersecurity-specialized AI foundation model** project, which includes agentic security service demonstration. KISIA (340 members) seeks to join. — [FN 2026-09-03](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202609031809097402); [AItimes](https://www.aitimes.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=214419); [Nate](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260914n23956)
- MSIT and KISA named Jiransoft's genAI plus email DLP-and-audit technology a 2026 "excellent information security technology". — [Dailysecu](https://www.dailysecu.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=207650)
- KISIA launched an AI Security Council in May 2025, first chaired by Eroun's CEO. A later "AI Security Intelligence Council" is chaired by Stealien's CEO. — [etnews](https://www.etnews.com/20250521000371); [Byline tag](https://byline.network/tag/%EC%8A%A4%ED%8B%B8%EB%A6%AC%EC%96%B8)
- N2SF was incorporated into the National Cybersecurity Guidelines in May 2026. Digital Daily notes that AI agents and MCP servers remain a **control gap** in zero-trust builds, and that most agents are not connected to corporate SSO and have no permission definitions. — [Digital Daily](https://www.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2026062621155026955)

### Inferences
- The FSI criteria (tool-permission control, human approval, audit) amount to a **de facto procurement spec** for agent permission and audit in Korean finance from 2027. Vendors that map products to the 17 items will have an advantage. No vendor was found claiming that mapping yet.
- KISA grants so far fund guardrails and red-teaming more than agent authorization. Apart from RaonSecure's physical-AI identity project, no dedicated "AI agent authorization/audit" national project was found.

### Gaps
- The FSI's 17 items in full are not public yet.
- No 2026 national R&D call titled "AI 에이전트 보안" was found apart from the physical-AI and cyber foundation-model projects. The Pangyo/Information Security Cluster and K-Cyber Security Challenge AI tracks were not covered.
